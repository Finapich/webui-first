About Angkor Taste

Nestled in the heart of Phnom Penh, Angkor Taste Restaurant was born from a passion for sharing the rich culinary heritage of Cambodia. We believe in the power of food to tell a story. Our chefs meticulously craft each dish using generations-old recipes and the freshest local ingredients, bringing you an authentic taste of Khmer tradition.

From our signature Fish Amok to the bustling flavors of Beef Lok Lak, every meal is a celebration of our culture. We invite you to join our family, experience our warm hospitality, and create lasting memories around our table.
